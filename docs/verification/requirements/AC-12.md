# AC-12 — Durable scheduler, races, restart, misfires and DST

Status: verified by executable local acceptance and focused regression checks;
exact committed-tree gates required before merge.

Requirement: `Scheduled occurrence runs once across workers, persists UTC next time, recovers misfire.`

## Product correction

Plan 08 requires a recorded reason when a nonexistent spring-forward local time
advances to the first valid instant. The prior adapter returned only the selected
UTC time, losing the nominal nonexistent wall-clock time. AC-12 adds a typed
recurrence result and optional `nonexistent_local_time` resolution containing the
nominal local time, original IANA zone, chosen UTC time and schema version.
The existing datetime facade, occurrence identity, recurrence version, earlier
fall-back fold and bounded 10,000-occurrence coalescing rules remain unchanged.

Configuration persists the pending selection. Processing preserves that exact
due selection, its next selection and every adjustment inside a coalesced missed
range. The first future adjustment belongs to the next selection, not the missed
range. Standalone ingress can retain the pending selection without inventing
unobserved advancement or range facts. Disable-only retains the pending evidence;
editing cron or timezone recomputes it even while the schedule is disabled.

Migration `0009` adds four nullable text columns without rebuilding tables,
backfilling old records or changing historical integrity material. Canonical JSON
snapshots participate in integrity and replay comparisons whenever present.
NULL means unknown historical evidence; an explicit empty array means an observed
range without adjustments. Claims, configuration CAS and advancement preserve
recorded evidence; recorded-to-NULL downgrades fail before any write.

Occurrence and advancement audits carry identical optional safe summaries only
when a due, interior or next selection has a gap. Fixed-size metadata retains
known/observed flags, due and next resolutions, count, first/last adjustment and
SHA256 of the complete calendar-only adjustment list. Full ranges stay in durable
occurrence storage. The existing audit byte/key/depth limits and central sealing
and redaction remain enforced. Ordinary and legacy audit shapes stay unchanged.
Committed-response replay verifies recorded facts against their sealed witnesses,
without asking a newer timezone database to regenerate historical gap reasons.
The pre-existing deterministic UTC/misfire replay check is retained separately.

## Composed default-runtime acceptance

The five cases in `test_ac_12_scheduler_recovery.py` use fresh migrated/seeded
SQLite, public configuration APIs with real If-Match revisions and scheduled
business input, default runtime composition and original scheduler/run workers.
They observe real provider methods; no positive-path claim, result or receipt is
fabricated. Input canaries must not appear in scheduler audit metadata.

The spring and fall cases reconstruct the pending runtime before racing two
independent engines. Both real due scans finish and release their original
transactions before the competing claim attempts proceed. One worker wins, one
loses, and one occurrence/work/run exists with the original due identity. A second
reconstruction before the real run worker produces one model artifact and zero
connector calls. Later repeated drains remain idle, including the second fall
fold. Persisted UTC advancement, original local representation and public audit
feed facts are checked against durable storage.

The skip/run_once cases genuinely claim and commit a two-minute lease, then close
the runtime before processing. Replacement workers remain idle before and at its
exact expiry. One microsecond later, two real workers race to recover it. The
March 7–9 range contains three due occurrences, including an interior March 8
02:30 local gap resolved to 03:00. Skip records one skipped occurrence and no
work/run. Run-once records one original-due catch-up and one post-restart model
artifact. Both retain the full gap evidence, advance to March 10, reject the old
claim fence and remain idle on repeated reconstruction.

The fifth case disables a configured spring-gap schedule, preserves its pending
selection, changes its timezone while disabled, reconstructs it, and re-enables
it. It proves the new UTC selection is coherent without stale gap evidence or
unintended work/provider calls.

## Scope and limits

Restart means in-process runtime shutdown/reconstruction and controlled claim
abandonment, not an OS-process kill. Separate transaction regressions inject
rollback and lost responses. Provider outputs are deterministic local mocks, not
real external effects or a universal distributed exactly-once guarantee.

The large audit-bound unit fixture uses synthetic historical calendar facts; it
does not claim a real timezone has daily gaps. Recorded evidence is validated
structurally, not re-resolved against current timezone rules. A changed UTC
projection can still fail the retained timestamp replay check closed.

Preflight caught a test-only barrier placed inside a SQLite immediate transaction;
the final barrier waits after the real read transaction exits. Audit assertions
use the actual pseudonymized worker correlation contract. Neither correction
replaces production operations with successful test doubles.

Repository-wide style inspection found unrelated pre-existing formatting/lint
issues. They are outside this requirement and are not silently repaired or claimed
clean; the declared style gates cover the changed source and relevant tests.
CI remains disabled and no workflow files are changed.
